# Project governance

**Hinterlib** is an open source software project. Its development is stewarded by Aquefir Consulting LLC (&ldquo;Aquefir&rdquo;), a North
Carolina limited liability company. Aquefir&rsquo;s agent is
[Alexander Nicholi][1]; therefore, the project is effectively governed
by them.

## Roles

Leadership recognises three different roles people may take in the
course of their involvement in the project, each with increasing levels
of discretion and trust:

1. The **contributor**, defined as anyone who has ever successfully
   contributed changes to the software
2. The **collaborator**, defined as someone with enhanced access control
   enabling them to make non-administrative changes without supervision
3. The **maintainer**, defined as the person or people responsible for
   integrating all contributions, moderating development discussions,
   setting project policy, and ensuring the project&rsquo;s health and
   availability to the public

The project has one (1) maintainer: **Alexander Nicholi**.

There are currently no active collaborators.

Consult the repository statistics for a complete list of contributors.

## General policy

1. All discussions must be **on-topic**.
	1. For all intents and purposes, on-topic means relating to the
	   project at hand.
2. All maintainers, collaborators and active contributors are expected
   to maintain a modicum of civility in whatever discussions they may
   come to participate in.
3. The [Code of Conduct][2] must be adhered to.
4. The [Contributing guide][3] must be followed when
   applicable.
